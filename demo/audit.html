    Drop a CSV route sheet here — one row per stop

    Export one row per stop, in visit order. You need the round it belongs to, the stop, its delivery window, and the travel time from the previous stop. That last column is the whole trick: checking a fixed round needs the legs you actually drive, not a full distance matrix, so a normal route sheet is enough. Headers can be named anything.

    Drop a rota CSV here — one row per shift

    Indicative mathematical check against configured rest and break rules (11h inter-shift rest, 20m break over 6h) — not legal advice. It compares times against the thresholds below and nothing more. It knows nothing of opt-outs, young workers, shift-change exceptions, compensatory rest or any collective agreement. A clear result is not a defence and a flagged one is not an offence. Rest and shift lengths are measured in clock time: on the night the clocks go forward, an 11-hour gap is only 10 real hours and is still reported as clear.

    One row per shift. You need who is working, when the shift starts and ends, and the break in minutes. A date column is used if present; otherwise put full date-times in the start and end columns. Night shifts that run past midnight are handled — an end earlier than its start is treated as the next day.
