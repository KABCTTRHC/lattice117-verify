    Columns are matched from your header row: the round, the stop, when its window opens and closes, and the travel time from the previous stop. Names can be anything.

    Indicative mathematical check against configured rest and break rules (11h inter-shift rest, 20m break over 6h) — not legal advice. It knows nothing of opt-outs, young workers, shift-change exceptions, compensatory rest or any collective agreement. Rest and shift lengths are measured in clock time: on the night the clocks go forward, an 11-hour gap is only 10 real hours and is still reported as clear.
