What the add-in does

Select your route or rota rows, including the header, and choose Schedule Check. It reports what proportion of your rounds can actually be run against their own time windows, names the stop that breaks, when it arrives, when the window shuts and by how much it is missed, and colours the failing rows in the sheet.

Your sheet

One row per stop, in visit order. Column names can be anything — they are matched from your header row. Five are required: the round, the stop, when its window opens, when it closes, and the travel time from the previous stop.

A sequence column is used if present; otherwise sheet order is taken as visit order.

Common questions

"Could not find these columns in your header row"

The matcher could not identify one of the five required columns. Check the header row is included in your selection and that each required column has a heading. Headings can be worded however you like.

"…is outside the Q16.16 representable range"

Values are held as fixed-point numbers representing −32,768 to +32,767. In minutes that caps a schedule at about 22 days; in seconds, about 9 hours. If you are working in seconds, switch the sheet to minutes. The add-in refuses out-of-range values by name rather than wrapping them, because a wrapped value produces a confident verdict about a schedule that was never actually checked.

My licence key is not accepted

Keys are checked by signature, so a truncated copy-paste is the usual cause — make sure you have the whole string including the part after the full stop. Keys also expire; the pane shows the expiry date once a key is applied. If it still fails, email us with the key and we will reissue.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the pane has opened, it makes no network requests at all. You can disconnect entirely and keep working.

Is my data uploaded?

No, and it cannot be — there is no server to upload it to. See the privacy policy.

What it deliberately does not do

It verifies time windows and travel times. It does not build routes, predict traffic, or judge whether a schedule is good — only whether it is possible. Vehicle capacity is not yet checked as a hard constraint.

The rota rest check is an indicative mathematical check against the rest windows you configure. It is not legal advice and is not a determination of compliance with the Working Time Regulations or any other law.
